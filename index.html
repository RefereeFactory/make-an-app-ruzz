The nuts and bolts

How it works

1

Browse & learn

The directory holds hand-written profiles of familiar birds. Search and filter run instantly in your browser. Tap a card for the full story, and use the ★ to save favourites.

2

Check the sky

The weather tool looks up your location through The Factory's places service, then fetches a live current-conditions reading from the free Open-Meteo service to give a real birding outlook.

3

It remembers

Favourites and journal entries are stored permanently in this app's own cloud notebook, shared by everyone who visits — so the record grows together over time.
